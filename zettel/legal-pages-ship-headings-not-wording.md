# Legal pages ship headings, not wording

The privacy and terms pages carry their section structure and no text.
Every unfilled section prints "Раздел не заполнен." in red.

Plausible filler would have been worse than nothing. A page that looks
like a privacy policy can be deployed unnoticed and then read as a real
promise about real data — a legal document is written per project and per
jurisdiction, and no starter can guess it.

Empty sections would have been worse too: silence looks like an oversight
in the layout rather than a task nobody did.
